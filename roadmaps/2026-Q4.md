# Roadmap for Q4 2026

## Nahara's Motion

We are now continuing our efforts in the great Nahara's Motion rewrite. However,
due to [unexpected event related to Deno][deno] (which has been our primary
development platform), we need to plan transitioning Nahara's Motion to other
runtime. The actual porting will happens next year, but right now, we need to
push Nahara's Motion to usable state first.

- [ ] Nahara's Motion Engine
- [ ] Motion Description Language
- [ ] Standard library for Nahara's Motion
- [ ] Nahara's Motion Editor
- [ ] First round of user testing

## Nahara's Sketchpad

There are no plans for Sketchpad in Q4 2026. It is still under construction, but
we hope to revisit Sketchpad next year, especially when we get to engineering a
new node-based engine for digital photo editing and painting.
